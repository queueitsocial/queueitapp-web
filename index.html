How it works

Three steps. One great night.

1

Pick a venue & drink

Browse bars near you, choose a drink from their real menu, and pick who you’re sending it to.

2

Pay securely

Check out with Apple Pay or any card. Stripe handles the payment — we never store your card details.

3

They show the QR

Your friend gets notified instantly. They show the bartender a QR code and the drink is poured.

4

Capture the moment

After redeeming, snap a photo and share it to the social feed. Your friends see the love in real time.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Queue It (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Queue It mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App. By using the App you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information: name, username, email address, and optional phone number
  • Profile information: bio, city, and profile photo
  • Payment information: processed securely by Stripe — we never store raw card numbers
  • Contacts: with your permission, we access your contacts only to find friends already on Queue It
  • Photos: drink photos you choose to post to the social feed
  • Push token: to send you drink notifications and friend activity alerts

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process drink orders and issue redemption codes to recipients
  • To send push notifications when you receive a drink or a friend request is accepted
  • To match you with friends via phone contacts (hashed and never stored on our servers)
  • To display your posts on the in-app social feed
  • To prevent fraud and verify users are 21 or older
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Payment Processing

All payments are handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not store your credit card, debit card, or bank account information. Stripe’s privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Stripe to process payments and issue refunds
  • With Expo (Expo Push Notifications) to deliver push notifications
  • With venue operators, solely to facilitate drink redemption
  • If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Contact Matching

If you grant contacts permission, phone numbers from your address book are used only to identify which of your contacts are already on Queue It. Numbers are hashed before transmission and are never stored or used for any other purpose.

6. Social Feed

Photos and captions you post to the Queue It feed are visible to other users of the App. You can delete your posts at any time from your profile.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing privacy@queueit.app. Payment records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by financial regulations.

8. Age Requirement

Queue It is intended solely for users who are 21 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 21. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately at privacy@queueit.app.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including TLS encryption in transit and secure, access-controlled databases. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@queueit.app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at privacy@queueit.app.

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from Queue It Social. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt-out.
